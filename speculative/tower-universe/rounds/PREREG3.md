Written 2026-11-06, before the round-3 numbers were computed. It adds reading O1. Eight readings in all, so a pass must

## The observer

- **Lives at** `N ≈ 8·10⁶⁰`.
- **Clock.** The tick, one integer per Planck time. The prime clock is reported too.
- **No external ruler.** The tower has no bound objects, so the growth `‖w_N‖ ∝ √N` is invisible from inside: S1 was an
  outside-only reading.
- **Light.** The zeros' modes `n^{iγ}`. A mode's phase advances by `γ/n` per tick: its frequency in the observer's own
  units.

## O1: redshift from the modes

A mode emitted at tick `n` and seen at tick `N` has its frequency lowered by `N/n` for every zero alike. So
`1 + z = N/n`, which gives `a ∝ t`.

On the prime clock the phase advances `≈ γ/τ` per prime, which gives the same `a ∝ t`.
