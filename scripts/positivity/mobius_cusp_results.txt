 (a) periodic v(X2)=v(X1), v'(X2)=v'(X1): both e^{+-irx} terms must vanish separately -> e^{irL}=1,
     r=2pi n/L. phi drops out: no zeta at all.
 (b) Mobius x->2c-x (y->Y^2/y, Y=sqrt(Y1Y2)), v(2c-x)=eps v(x): matching coefficients gives
     phi(s) = eps Y^{2s-1}, eps=+1 (Neumann-type) or -1 (Dirichlet-type, = pseudo-Laplacian).
 (c) twisted: phi(s) = e^{i alpha} Y^{2s-1}.
 All are restrictions of a self-adjoint problem: |phi|=1=|Y^{2s-1}| on Re s=1/2 gives real r.
